## V4 clustered-contact performance fix

After the polish round, a focused stress check reproduced a performance collapse when many enemies occupied
the same contact area around a player. The issue was traced to `Enemy._apply_separation()`: every clustered
enemy evaluated every nearby clustered neighbor, creating near-quadratic physics-frame work.

### What changed

- Bounded enemy separation to at most 8 valid neighbors per enemy.
- Staggered separation updates over 4 physics frames using each enemy's existing frame offset.
- Cached the last separation push so skipped frames preserve movement continuity instead of disabling
  separation outright.

### Validation

- Temporary clustered-contact stress scene:
  - Before fix: `100` enemies `avg_fps=3.5`, `150` enemies `avg_fps=1.5`, `200` enemies `avg_fps=1.0`.
  - After fix: `100` enemies `avg_fps=144.6`, `150` enemies `avg_fps=144.1`, `200` enemies `avg_fps=119.3`.
