## Cover-only enemy cap (Phase 6 residual physics)

- The residual `flowfield_stress` bottleneck is `move_and_slide`/separation cost from enemies bunching around
  physical cover — it does not occur in open/hazard rooms (`entity_ramp` passes at 200).
- Added a cover-only concurrent-enemy cap instead of a global cap or a physics rewrite:
  - `WaveDirector.MAX_OBSTACLE_ENEMIES = 160`, gated on `CoopManager.has_flow_obstacles()`.
  - New `CoopManager.get_live_enemy_count()` accessor.
  - All three spawn loops break before the shooter pick when `live + pending >= 160` (no budget-reservation leak).
- Open rooms stay uncapped (swarm power-fantasy intact); density scaling still fills to the cap and refills as
  enemies die. Measured headroom: 150 enemies = 110fps, so a 160 ceiling holds comfortably ≥60fps.
- Perf probes (`flowfield_stress`, `where:<id>`) inject 200 directly and intentionally bypass the cap.
