### Stutter Investigation

- Pre-fix `boss:warden --players=2 --build=heavy`: `warden_charge_combo` marker around `62.909 ms`; worst sampled frame `178.195 ms`.
- Pre-fix Hydra/Hive heavy profiles showed smaller first-sample spikes (`38.051 ms` / `35.458 ms`), while Pulsar stayed below `15 ms`.
- Root cause matched cold-load/first-use combat VFX/projectile paths, especially Warden charge combo.
- Added combat VFX/projectile prewarm at room start.
- Review follow-up found the first prewarm was incomplete because invisible/off-screen samples do not guarantee CanvasItem draw/shader warmup.
- Fresh profiling with the old prewarm still showed Warden `88.110 ms` max frame and Hydra `38.749 ms` max frame.
- Reworked prewarm to render low-alpha on-screen samples for one frame, including large boss telegraph/shockwave rings, hazards, screen flash, projectile/trail samples, and common boss-minion enemy visuals.
- Corrected `PerfRunner` worst-frame ranking to use actual frame delta (`delta * 1000`) instead of mixing in stale process/physics monitor values.
- Final heavy 2P post-fix profiles:
  - Warden: `23.144 max_frame_ms`
  - Hydra: `13.507 max_frame_ms`
  - Hive: `5.643 max_frame_ms`
  - Pulsar: `12.925 max_frame_ms`
- Post-fix Warden base: all markers exercised; max frame `18.744 ms`.
