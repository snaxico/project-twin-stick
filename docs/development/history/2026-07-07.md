# 2026-07-07 — V4 Round 2 rebalance, Rooms, flow-field pathfinding, early-difficulty ease

## What shipped
- **V4 Round 2 rebalance** (`844d3a3`) — coordinated "rein in the player, harden threats" pass, all numbers
  designed collaboratively and locked in `docs/development/v4-round-2-plan.md`:
  - Perf caps: `MAX_ACTIVE_HOMING=40`, `MAX_ACTIVE_SUMMONS=5` (drop-oldest).
  - Bugs: ult charge `KILL 0.05->0.02` (~1/room); enemy projectiles persist to wall/player/summon + arena-bounds
    despawn; per-class HUD indicator; ignite clarity; flamethrower flame-cone feel.
  - Balance: Arc Wand nerf; Summon dmg/interval; boss HP ~2x; Tank overshield `0.65->0.25`/decay `5->9`/heal
    `5->2`; ability cooldowns +25%; heat decay `14->6`/delay `1.5`; HP-pickup drop `0.06->0.03`.
  - Rooms: hybrid curated archetypes (`data/room_archetypes.json`, 6 types, 2 combat choices, <=2 on-theme
    modifiers, archetype-aware dedupe, density-profile spawn scaling), replacing random modifier rolls.
- **Element delivery gate** (`c9b9953`) — fire_trail/freeze_shot/poison now `requires:["projectile"]` (the
  shipped dedupe alone missed the flamethrower); closes the reported "Fire Bullets on flamethrower" bug.
- **Flow-field pathfinding** (`f2f4ea5`) — full implementation of `docs/development/v4-arena-pathfinding-plan.md`:
  `FlowField.gd`, movement/aim split across all enemy types, layer-1 obstacles with lifecycle + spawn-safety +
  connectivity validation, indexed profiling targets.
- **Early-difficulty / perf ease** (`da160bb`) — gentler early density ramp (burst 6->4/5->3, interval
  0.58->0.90s early, density mult 1.15->1.0 early) + spitter `fire_interval 1.35->1.8`, from playtest feedback
  that the start was too dense while weak.

## Open / carried forward
- **⚠ Flow-field perf blocker:** `flowfield_stress` = 16 fps at 200 enemies (vs 142 baseline). Per-enemy
  `sample()` (nearest-reachable flood-fill + 84px inflation) is the cost. The flow field is **off in normal
  play** (no room authors obstacles), so it doesn't affect the current build — but it **must be optimized
  before obstacles are authored** (precompute nearest-reachable at build; reduce inflation).
- Round 2 wants a live 1P/2P feel check across the four classes, especially the eased early game.

## Process notes
- Codex promoted V4 to canonical (main checkout, `v4/class-system`); the `_v4` worktree is gone.
- Reviews were tight this round — the biggest catch was that enemies were never "ghost mode"
  (`collision_layer/mask=1`), which reshaped the pathfinding design (keep physics + flow for routing).
