## Round 14 Playtest + Fixes

Ran the manual round-14 playtest. Three findings, all fixed in the working tree and headless-validated.

### Findings → fixes

- **Aim line too big / placative** — `Player.gd` aim reticle: width `4.0 → 2.0`, alpha `0.78 → 0.30`,
  length cap `340 → 170`, and dropped the chevron arrowhead (now a thin faint short line).
- **Beam only flashed for milliseconds** — `CoopManager.gd` beam visual: each fire tick drew the beam
  then immediately tweened alpha to 0 over `0.12s`, and stacked tweens made it flicker. Now the beam
  stays solid while firing (alpha `0.42 → 0.55`); a new `_update_beam_visual_timeouts()` sweep in
  `_physics_process` hides it `0.16s` after firing stops. Continuous beam that clears when you stop.
- **HP should be the circle, only after damage** — `PlayerCombatIndicator.gd`: replaced the small
  rectangular health bar with a circular health ring (radius `24`) around the player that only draws
  below full HP (i.e. after taking damage); pulses green→red at low HP, red while downed. Removed the
  now-unused `BAR_WIDTH` / `BAR_HEIGHT` constants.
